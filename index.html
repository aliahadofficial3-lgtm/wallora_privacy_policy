Privacy Policy Effective: September 2026 Version 1.0

1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Wallora ("we", "our", or "us"). We build premium, privacy-conscious Android applications designed to transform your smartphone and tablet displays with stunning 4K, Ultra HD, and AMOLED wallpapers.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wallora collects, uses, handles, and protects information when you install and use our Android mobile application. We are committed to complete transparency, user privacy by design, and strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Core Privacy Principle: Wallora does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal identity details. Your favorites, download histories, and searches remain securely stored on your own device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To respect your privacy, Wallora operates without collecting intrusive personal data:

  • No Account Registration: You are never required to log in, provide an email address, enter your phone number, or authenticate via social media.
  • No Sensitive Personal Telemetry: We do not collect your physical real-time GPS location, contacts list, call logs, SMS messages, audio recordings, or camera feeds.
  • No Biometric Data: Wallora does not scan, request, or handle biometric data (such as fingerprint or face unlock information).

3. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

Wallora provides rich features while keeping your personal preferences isolated within your phone or tablet:

  • Saved Favorites & Collections: Wallpapers that you bookmark as favorites are stored locally in an Android Room SQLite database on your device. This data is never synchronized with or uploaded to any remote server.
  • Recent Search Queries: Search terms typed into the exploration bar are stored solely in your local device cache to speed up repeated queries. You can clear this history anytime in the Settings menu.
  • Downloaded Wallpapers (MediaStore API): When you choose to download a wallpaper in Original 4K or 1080p, the image file is written directly to your device's standard public directory (Pictures/Wallora) using Android's modern, privacy-friendly MediaStore API.
  • Cached Thumbnails: To save mobile data bandwidth and provide instantaneous scrolling, thumbnails are cached locally on your device via Coil. You can wipe this cache at any moment inside the app settings.

4. Android Permissions & Hardware Access

Wallora requests only the minimal set of Android system permissions strictly necessary to execute its core functionality:

Permission Type Purpose
SET_WALLPAPER Normal (Install-time) Allows Wallora to apply your selected wallpaper to your device's Home Screen, Lock Screen, or both simultaneously via the official Android WallpaperManager.
INTERNET Normal (Install-time) Enables fetching catalog metadata, discovering trending categories, and streaming high-resolution wallpaper graphics from secure Content Delivery Networks.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Detects whether you are connected to Wi-Fi or cellular networks, enabling the "Download on Wi-Fi Only" bandwidth conservation setting.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Used optionally to alert you when a new Daily Wallpaper drops or fresh curated collections arrive. You can revoke this anytime in Settings.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Legacy (API ≤ 28 only) Included with android:maxSdkVersion="28" strictly for legacy Android 9 and older devices. Android 10+ uses Scoped Storage (MediaStore) without requiring broad storage permissions.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Wallora is offered free of charge and is supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We implement the official Google Mobile Ads SDK. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information to deliver contextual or personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers, including the Google Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Coarse device characteristics (device model, screen resolution, operating system version).
  • App performance and crash diagnostic metrics.
  • Coarse location or IP address to determine general region or country for relevant ad delivery.

Google's handling of ad data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)

Wallpapers showcased in Wallora are curated from high-quality photographic sources and partner CDNs (including Unsplash and Pexels APIs). When downloading or previewing wallpapers, standard HTTPS network requests are routed to these CDNs, transmitting standard HTTP request headers (such as IP address and User-Agent) necessary to fulfill media delivery.

6. Data Retention & User Control Rights

Because Wallora stores your application preferences locally, you maintain total sovereignty over your data:

  • Clear Cache: In Settings → Storage & Cache → Clear Image Cache, you can instantly flush all cached temporary images to reclaim storage space.
  • Clear Search History: In Settings → Storage & Cache → Clear Search History, you can permanently wipe all recorded search queries.
  • Remove Saved Favorites: You can un-favorite any wallpaper individually from the Favorites tab or remove downloaded files directly from your gallery.
  • Full Deletion: Clearing Wallora app storage via Android OS Settings or uninstalling the app permanently erases the local Room database and all associated application data.

7. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you are entitled to specific rights under the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Since we do not host personal data on external servers, all data is retained directly in your possession on your device.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Easily accomplished at any time by clearing application data or uninstalling Wallora.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: Wallora does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for monetary or valuable consideration.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Wallora does not target and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU member states). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to address the concern.

9. Security of Your Information

We prioritize security by utilizing Android's sandboxed application architecture. All network communications fetching wallpapers, metadata, and ads utilize Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption). Local databases reside in private, application-sandboxed storage inaccessible to other installed applications.
